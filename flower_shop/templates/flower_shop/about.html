Our story

Petals and Poetry began with a simple idea: flowers and words belong together. What started as a small corner stall is now a cozy shop where every bouquet comes with a line of poetry.

We work with local growers whenever we can, so our flowers are fresh, fairly priced, and kind to the earth.

What we believe

Small gestures matter. A bouquet can say what words cannot, and a good book can keep you company long after the petals fade.
